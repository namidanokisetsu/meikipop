Download the installer for your computer below. Python and command-line setup are not required.

- **Windows 10/11 (x64):** run `Meikipop-2.1.0-windows-x64-setup.exe`.
- **Mac with Apple silicon:** open `meikipop.macos.arm64.dmg` and drag Meikipop to Applications.
- **Mac with Intel:** open `meikipop.macos.x86_64.dmg` and drag Meikipop to Applications.

The first-launch wizard helps choose a language, add dictionaries and configure shortcuts. Dictionaries, OCR models and optional local translation/base-form models are downloaded from inside the app. No personal dictionaries, settings or model weights are included in the installers. Existing user data is preserved on upgrade and uninstall.

These builds are not publisher-signed or Apple-notarized. Windows may show SmartScreen; macOS may require allowing the app in System Settings → Privacy & Security after the first launch attempt. Screen recognition and global shortcuts on macOS also require the relevant Screen Recording and Accessibility permissions.

SHA-256 checksum files accompany the installers.

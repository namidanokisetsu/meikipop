Turkish morphology incorporates code from Anki Miner by 0xzerolight and contributors.
https://github.com/0xzerolight/anki_miner/tree/808b5208bf93d919b3f18ea3aba66db5fd7fd0ea
Files: anki_miner/languages/tr/analyzer.py, morphology.py, tokenizer.py.
License: GPL-3.0. The accompanying GPL-3.0.txt contains the license.

Meikipop adapts the analyzer's imports and the context selection to its source spans,
retains ranked alternatives and full morpheme paths for dictionary lookup and display,
and bounds/caches parsing. Desktop builds precompute the lexicon family rankings.
Zeyrek 0.1.3 parsing and suffix-generation portions retain the MIT license in zeyrek.txt.
